
# MEYRA — BRAND IDENTITY

## Brand
Name: Meyra
Category: Islamic lifestyle and worship companion
Primary market: Türkiye
Languages: Turkish, English, Arabic

## Mission
Help people build a more consistent, peaceful and
organized spiritual routine through technology.

## Positioning
Meyra is more than a prayer times application.
It is an all-in-one Islamic lifestyle companion.

## Core features
- Prayer times and Adhan notifications
- Qibla finder
- Quran reading and audio
- Quran learning
- Dhikr counter
- Prayer and Qada tracking
- Hatim tracking
- AI-powered Islamic assistant
- Duas, Hadiths and Asmaul Husna
- Islamic books and reflection content
- Nearby mosques
- Religious tools and notifications

## Audience
Primary: Turkish-speaking Muslims
Secondary: English and Arabic-speaking Muslims

## Brand personality
- Peaceful
- Trustworthy
- Minimal
- Modern
- Respectful
- Helpful

## Content principles
- Never invent Quran verses or Hadiths.
- Verify religious claims using reliable sources.
- Avoid fear-based marketing.
- Never exploit religious emotions.
- Do not make unsupported accuracy claims.
- Do not promise guaranteed spiritual outcomes.
- Prioritize useful and shareable content.

## Growth objective
Primary KPI: Qualified app downloads.
Secondary KPIs:
- First app launch
- Onboarding completion
- User activation
- Retention
- Premium conversion
