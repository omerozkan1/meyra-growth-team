## Account management rules

- These are the official Meyra accounts.
- Never create duplicate social accounts.
- Analyze each platform independently.
- Preserve brand identity across all platforms.
- Adapt video hooks, captions and hashtags per platform.
- Track follower growth, reach, views, engagement and profile visits.
- Measure store clicks and attributed app downloads.
- Never publish without explicit owner approval.

## Instagram strategy

- Reels for discovery.
- Stories for daily engagement.
- Carousels for educational content.
- Optimize profile bio and store links.

## TikTok strategy

- Strong hooks in the first 2 seconds.
- Native vertical video editing.
- Test different video lengths.
- Identify high-retention content.

## YouTube strategy

- YouTube Shorts discovery.
- Search-friendly titles.
- Consistent thumbnails for long-form videos.
- Analyze watch time and subscriber conversion.

## Reporting

Produce separate platform reports and a combined weekly growth report.
Identify which content generates actual app downloads.
