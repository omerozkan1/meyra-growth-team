# Brand: Meyra

## Core
- **Name:** Meyra
- **Website tagline (TR):** "Kendine yeniden başla." · EN: "Start over, renew yourself." · AR: "ابدأ من جديد، جدد نفسك."
- **Descriptor:** "Senin manevi asistanın" (your spiritual assistant)
- **Primary promise for content (proposed, owner to confirm):** *Calm, uncluttered daily worship: prayer times, Quran and dhikr in one simple app.* This combines the website's calm and minimal tone with the stores' practical utility.
- **Proof points that are publicly verifiable:** 4.5 ★ Play (46 reviews), 4.4 ★ App Store TR (21 ratings) as of 2026-11-14. Re-check before quoting. Never round up.

## Voice
| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Warm, calm, gentle ("sen" form, as on the website) | Preachy, guilt-inducing, fear-based |
| Short sentences, everyday Turkish | Heavy Ottoman/Arabic jargon without explanation |
| Invite: "küçük bir adımla başla" (start with a small step) | Pressure: "hemen indir yoksa…" (download now or else…) |
| Practical, honest about free vs Premium | Hype: "en iyi", "devrim", "sıfır sapma" |
| Respect all Muslims' practice levels; welcoming to beginners | Judge practice levels, sectarian takes |

## Visual direction (until the owner supplies a brand kit)
- Calm palette: deep night blue / soft green / warm off-white. **[A]** Placeholder until the real colours are confirmed.
- Real app screen recordings > stock footage. Stock footage only from free-licence libraries (Pexels/Pixabay); store the source link in the video file.
- Nature, light, hands with tesbih, mosque silhouettes. **No faces of real people without consent. No depiction of prophets.**
- Text overlays: max ~7 words per frame, placed inside the platform safe zones (top 12% and bottom 20% stay clear).
