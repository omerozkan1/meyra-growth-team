# 30-Day Organic Acquisition Strategy (2026-11-16 → 2026-12-15)

## Objective
More **qualified organic installs** in Turkey. "Qualified" = an install followed by first launch and an activation action (see `tracking/measurement-plan.md`).

## Starting point (2026-11-14, public data)
- Play: 1K+ installs, 4.5 ★ (46). App Store TR: 4.4 ★ (21).
- No web analytics, no store campaign links, social accounts not yet linked to this workspace.
- **So the first goal is to establish a baseline. Hard install targets are set at the end of week 1**, not invented now.

## Bets (ranked by expected impact ÷ effort)
1. **Short vertical video, 1 master edit → 3 platforms** (IG Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts). Turkish, 15–35 s, real app footage.
2. **Seasonal relevance:** *Üç Aylar* begin around mid-December 2026 (Regaib Kandili expected ~10–11 Dec 2026; **verify on the Diyanet dini günler calendar**). Winter = short days and early yatsı, so prayer-time reminders matter more.
3. **Store conversion quick wins** (ASO): screenshots/captions, consistent naming, reply to all reviews. Drafts only; the owner applies them.
4. **Website hygiene for SEO and attribution** (robots/sitemap/hreflang, localised meta, GA4, UTM forwarding). Proposed as a ticket for the owner.

## Content pillars
| Pillar | Share | Purpose | Example angles |
|---|---|---|---|
| **P1 Manevi farkındalık** (spiritual awareness) | 35% | Reach + saves/shares; builds an audience that trusts the brand | A verse plus a calm reflection, "kendine yeniden başla" micro-stories, Üç Aylar intentions |
| **P2 Pratik ibadet araçları** (practical worship tools) | 35% | Search + utility, high saves; solves a real problem (app shown naturally) | Finding qibla in a hotel, tesbihat steps, winter prayer-time reminders, starting the Quran from zero |
| **P3 Meyra ürün demoları** (product demos) | 30% | Conversion; direct installs | 30-second setup, zikirmatik night mode, Learn Quran first lesson, hatim tracking, Meyra Asistan |

Every P1/P2 video ends with a *soft* CTA ("Meyra'da bunu kolayca yapabilirsin, link profilde"). Every P3 video ends with a *direct* CTA ("Ücretsiz indir, link profilde").

## Cadence (realistic for a solo owner with an approval step)
- **5 videos/week** (Mon–Fri), each cross-posted to Reels + TikTok + Shorts = 15 posts/week.
- **Batch rhythm:** Sunday is the approval session (the owner approves 5 concepts in about 20 min) → Mon/Tue batch filming of screen recordings → daily posting by the owner at the suggested slot.
- Suggested posting windows (TR time, **[A]** to be tuned with data): 12:30–13:30 (öğle break), 20:30–22:00 (after yatsı). Cuma: before 12:00.
- Daily 15 min: the `/daily-growth` skill drafts comment replies and the next-day plan.

## Phases
| Week | Dates | Focus | Output |
|---|---|---|---|
| W1 | 16–22 Nov | Setup + baseline + first 5 videos | Owner links accounts and grants read-only analytics; videos V01–V05 posted; baseline metrics recorded |
| W2 | 23–29 Nov | Test hooks; first ASO drafts | V06–V10 posted; hook A/B (same video, two hooks, on TikTok vs IG); store listing proposals |
| W3 | 30 Nov–6 Dec | Double down on the winning pillar/format; Üç Aylar prep | 5 videos (2 remakes of winners), Üç Aylar series scripted |
| W4 | 7–13 Dec | **Üç Aylar launch moment** | Regaib Kandili content (date verified), "Üç Aylar hedefim" series using worship/hatim tracking |
| D29–30 | 14–15 Dec | Review + next 30-day plan | `/weekly-report` + retrospective; decide EN expansion timing |

## Success metrics (targets set after the W1 baseline)
| Level | Metric | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Output | Videos published/week (goal 5), approval turnaround (< 48 h) | content files |
| Attention | Views, 3-s hold rate, avg % watched, saves, shares | platform insights (manual export) |
| Intent | Profile visits → link clicks (bio CTR) | platform insights |
| Store | Product page views, conversion rate, installs (TR) by source | ASC / Play Console |
| Quality | First opens, activation rate, D1/D7 retention | Firebase / GA4 (if connected) |

**Decision rules:**
- A format with < 1.5× the account's median views after 3 posts is dropped.
- A format with ≥ 2× median views is remade within 7 days with a new hook.
- Weekly KPI: *qualified installs per published video*. Proxy until Firebase is connected: store installs (TR) per video.

## Supporting formats (from `social-accounts.md`, low effort, reuse video assets)
- Instagram **Stories**: daily, e.g. a poll or question sticker plus a repost of the day's Reel (owner posts).
- Instagram **carousels**: one per week from the best P2 topic (e.g. "Seyahatte kıble: 3 yol"), as a Canva Free template.
- YouTube: search-friendly Shorts titles now; long-form with consistent thumbnails after day 30.
- Reporting: per-platform sections plus a combined weekly report (see `/weekly-report`).

## Out of scope for these 30 days
Paid ads, influencer payments, EN/AR content production (we only prepare the plan), app changes.
